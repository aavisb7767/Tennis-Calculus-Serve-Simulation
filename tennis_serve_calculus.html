Optimization Mathematical Formulation
Position (Projectile Motion):
x(t) = v₀·cos(θ)·t
y(t) = h₀ + v₀·sin(θ)·t - ½gt²
Velocity (Derivatives):
vₓ(t) = dx/dt = v₀·cos(θ)
vᵧ(t) = dy/dt = v₀·sin(θ) - gt
v(t) = √(vₓ² + vᵧ²)
With Air Resistance:
dvₓ/dt = -(k/m)·vₓ·v
dvᵧ/dt = -g - (k/m)·vᵧ·v
where k = ½ρCₐA, v = |velocity|
Velocity Components vs Time
Derivative Instantaneous Rate of Change
Velocity is the derivative of position: v = dx/dt. Acceleration is the derivative of velocity: a = dv/dt.
Integral Accumulation
Distance traveled is the integral of speed: d = ∫|v(t)|dt. Work done equals ∫F·ds.
